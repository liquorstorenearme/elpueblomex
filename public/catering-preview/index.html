The details

Minimum
40-guest minimum per event.
Lead time
Place orders at least 96 hours (4 days) ahead. Shorter notice is subject to approval and kitchen capacity.
Deposit
50% deposit confirms your booking; the balance is due 96 hours before the event.
Cancellation
Full refund 7+ days out. Inside 7 days the deposit is retained; inside 96 hours the order is non-refundable (food is already sourced).
Service charge
A 20% service charge applies to the food subtotal, covering on-site staff and gratuity.
Tax
California sales tax applies to the food subtotal plus service charge, at the rate for the event's county.
Delivery & travel
A distance-based travel fee is set per event by the servicing location and billed separately.
